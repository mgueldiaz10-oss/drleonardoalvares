Dr. Leonardo Álvarez Valtierra

Ph.D., Full Professor · University of Guanajuato
SNI Level II Ph.D. Univ. of Pittsburgh (2007) High-Resolution Laser Spectroscopy Gas Phase & Supersonic Jet

I am currently interested in laser spectroscopy of molecular systems in the gas phase. Most of my work focuses on the analyses of high resolution electronic spectra of relevant molecules in a supersonic beam, using either rigid or pseudo-rigid rotor Hamiltonians for both electronic states involved. From there, we are able to derive inertial parameters which reveal the structure of the sample traveling in the jet. Furthermore, we are trying to exploit Stark effect measurements, as well as laser ablation techniques for temperature-labile molecules (biomolecules); from which, we might be able to extract structural information and dipole moment components of relevant biomolecules in the gas phase.

Our Team

Molecular Spectroscopy Research Group · UG Principal Investigator, Graduate Fellows & Student Researchers

Cultivating World-Class Experimentalists

The Molecular Spectroscopy Laboratory serves as an academic incubator where students actively participate in the development, calibration, and operation of ultra-high precision optical instrumentation. Our members do not merely collect data; they master the fundamentals of vacuum physics, non-linear optics, pulsed laser synchronization, and rotational quantum mechanics.

Each researcher is mentored to develop independent analytical thinking, translating complex high-resolution spectroscopic signatures into geometric confirmations of conformational structures, internal rotation torsional barriers, and intermolecular forces.

Laboratory & Experimental Facilities

Premier scientific infrastructure distributed across five dedicated branches for the quantum investigation of matter.

Five Specialized Scientific Branches

To explore in depth our laser cavities, Spectra Physics laser systems, differential vacuum apparatus, quantum simulations, and technical engineering staff, visit our dedicated laboratory page with independent branching views.

Branch 01

High Resolution Laser Lab

Spectra Physics Millennia 532 nm pump, Matisse DS ring dye laser cavity, and Wavetrain UV SHG.

Branch 02

Molecular Beam Machine

Stainless steel 3-chamber differential vacuum system ($10^{-5}$ to $10^{-7}$ Torr) with diffusion pumps and quartz nozzle.

Branch 03

Molecular Quantum Computing

Geometry optimization and inertial parameter prediction via Gaussian 16 and proprietary code (Deamon).

Branch 04

Photocatalysis & Degradation

Photocatalytic degradation of organic pollutants with real-time UV laser and spectrophotometric kinetics.

Branch 05

Lab Technician & Support

Supervised by Dr. Eduardo Montes Ramírez (SNI I), managing Class IV laser safety and vacuum protocols.
